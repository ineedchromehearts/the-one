About Me

From a young age, I’ve had a strong passion for drawing and sketching, which sparked my interest in design and creativity. Over time, that passion evolved into a dedication to crafting user-centered digital experiences that are both functional and visually engaging. As an aspiring Experience Architect and Product Designer, I focus on UX research, web development, and creative problem-solving. My background includes working on a wide range of projects—from branding and graphic design to collaborative client work—where I’ve developed a strong understanding of how thoughtful design can improve everyday interactions. This portfolio showcases my growth as a designer and my commitment to creating intuitive, impactful solutions. Let’s connect: I’m always open to new opportunities, collaborations, and conversations that spark innovation and growth.
